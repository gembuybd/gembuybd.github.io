Who we are

GemBuybd is a small online shop in Bangladesh that sells shoe insoles through its Facebook Page, GemBuybd on Facebook.

Our Facebook app

We use our own Meta app ("gembuybd") only to manage our own Facebook Page: to publish posts and reels, to read the Page's statistics and ad results, to manage our ads, and to read and reply to messages sent to the Page. The app does not use Facebook Login and does not collect data from anyone's personal Facebook account.

Information we use

When you order, you send us your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you want, usually in Messenger. We use this only to confirm and deliver your order and to handle exchanges or returns.

Who we share it with

We give your name, phone number and address to the courier company that delivers your order. We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep order details only as long as we need them to deliver the order and handle any exchange or return, and to keep basic sales records.

Deleting your data

To have your information deleted, send a message to the GemBuybd Facebook Page saying "Delete my data", with the phone number you used to order. We will delete your order details and confirm in Messenger within 30 days. Messages you sent us stay in your own Messenger until you delete them there.
